🛡️ The ₹1,000 Capital Intraday Playbook

  • Ticket Price Discipline: Only trade stocks priced under ₹300 (e.g. PNB, CANBK, TATASTEEL, POWERGRID). High-priced stocks prevent proper position sizing.
  • Brokerage & Friction Awareness: Ensure your broker charges percentage-based brokerage (0.05%), avoiding flat ₹20 fee traps on tiny accounts.
  • Opening 15-Minute Rule: Never trade between 9:15 AM and 9:30 AM IST. Let initial institutional knee-jerk moves settle and establish opening range boundaries.
  • Hard System Stop-Loss: Never rely on "mental stops". Place a Stop-Loss Limit (SL-L) order as soon as your order fills.
  • Trailing to Breakeven: When the trade achieves Target 1 (+1.5R), immediately move your stop-loss to entry price for a completely risk-free session.
